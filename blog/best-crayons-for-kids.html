What to Look For When Buying Crayons

Age and barrel size: Match the crayon size to your child's grip development. Jumbo for under 2, large for ages 2–4, standard from around age 4 and up.

Washability: Always choose washable for children under 6. Once they're reliably coloring on paper only, non-washable becomes an option — but washable is still safer for furniture and clothing.

Color count: More isn't always better for young children. A toddler overwhelmed by 64 colors will freeze up; a 6-year-old with only 8 colors will feel creatively limited. Match the count to the child's age and attention span.

Brand quality: Crayola consistently produces more vibrant, longer-lasting crayons than budget alternatives. The price difference is small enough that we recommend Crayola for most families.

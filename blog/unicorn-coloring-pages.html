Fun Facts About Unicorns

Magical unicorn trivia

  • Unicorns appear in the mythology of ancient Greece, China, and medieval Europe — making them one of the most universal mythical creatures in history.
  • Scotland's national animal is the unicorn — a symbol of purity, power, and independence since the 12th century.
  • In medieval legends, a unicorn's horn (called an "alicorn") was believed to have the power to purify poisoned water.
  • The word "unicorn" comes from the Latin unicornis, meaning "one horn."
  • Unicorns appear in the King James Bible — though scholars believe the original Hebrew word referred to a wild ox, not a magical horse.

Coloring Tips for Magical Creatures

Unicorn and fantasy coloring is where creativity truly has no limits. Here are some techniques to try:

  • Rainbow everything: Use MagicPencil's gradient tool to give wings, manes, and tails a multi-color gradient effect — section by section.
  • Pastel palette: Soft pinks, lilacs, mint greens, and baby blues create a dreamy, magical feel.
  • Glitter simulation: Apply the dots pattern fill over a light color for a sparkly effect that mimics glitter.
  • Bold contrast: Pair a bright magenta body with a deep indigo background for a striking, dramatic look.
